# Worked examples

These short examples illustrate the intended judgment style. They are not exhaustive policies.

## 1. Repetition without progress

**Pattern:** The user and assistant repeatedly say that AI changes how people understand themselves, but no mechanism, boundary condition, example, or consequence is added.

**State:** `DIMINISHING RETURNS`

**Possible notice:** “We’ve returned to the same central claim several times, but the explanation hasn’t changed much. A useful next step would be to name one mechanism and test it against a counterexample.”

## 2. Productive uncertainty

**Pattern:** A discussion of AI and child development has no final answer yet, but it distinguishes age of first use, privacy expectations, family context, and perceived understanding.

**State:** `PRODUCTIVE UNCERTAINTY`

**Response:** Continue mapping the variables or turn one into a researchable question. Do not call the discussion stagnant merely because it remains unresolved.

## 3. Deliberate practice

**Pattern:** A learner repeats a pronunciation exercise and receives increasingly precise feedback.

**State:** `ADVANCING`

**Reason:** The task is repetitive by design, and performance is improving. Novelty is not the success criterion.

## 4. Emotional processing

**Pattern:** A user returns to a painful interaction and gradually identifies the boundary that felt crossed.

**State:** `ADVANCING` or `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`, depending on context.

**Reason:** Repetition may help the user clarify their experience. Do not interrupt simply to maximize information density.

## 5. A claim becomes testable

**Pattern:** A repeated high-level idea is refined into a causal mechanism and a longitudinal study that could disconfirm it.

**State:** `ADVANCING`

**Reason:** The same theme now has a more precise and testable form.

## 6. Goal achieved

**Pattern:** The user asked for a project plan. Scope, deliverables, risks, and next steps have been covered; the last few turns only paraphrase them.

**State:** `READY TO CLOSE`

**Possible notice:** “The requested plan now appears complete. I can consolidate it into a final checklist rather than restate the same points.”
